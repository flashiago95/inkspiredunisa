The question for this board

UNISA has taught Africa at a distance for generations. What if that distance had a front door?

Gap one

A place to be supported

Distance students study at kitchen tables and on phones. When they fall behind, nobody sees it until results are published.

Gap two

Laboratories for research

Marine science, geoscience and pharmacy need equipment, data and supervision that a screen alone cannot provide.

Gap three

A meeting point with industry

Employers, ports, mines and clinics need somewhere to meet the graduates they will hire and the research they will fund.

The model

One UNISA. Three sites. One system.

Each site anchors a catalytic niche its region already lives by, and is sized to the community it serves. All three share one design language and one digital backbone run from the main campus.

Risks the board should expect us to manage

Risk and response
Cost overrun
Fixed kit of parts specification, independent cost plan at every gate, no construction funding before the business case.
Low utilisation
Bookable spaces with live utilisation data. The Centre is not approved until the Hub meets agreed targets.
Technology ageing
Open standards, a shared backbone managed centrally and technology refresh priced into operating costs.
Weak local relevance
Each site is tied to a regional niche and to named industry and community partners before design is final.
The decision we request

Approve funding to take the Hub through detailed design and a full business case.

This is the first gated phase of One UNISA. It commits the university to evidence, not to construction.
